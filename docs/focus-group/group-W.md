# Focus group W (wealthy)

### W-01 · Yoav Ben-Ami, 44, tech founder (post-exit, now angel investor) · customer · tech 5 · unimpressed
- **Profile:** Sold his startup two years ago, lives in Baka, two kids, secular. Coffee on Emek Refaim most mornings, pays with Apple Pay and never thinks about the price. Judges every app as a product person: speed, polish, data handling.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use, price)
- **First impression:** Strong brand: the lime onboarding and the stacked wallet look like a real product, not a municipal pilot. But a free coffee is not a reason for me to install anything.
- **Liked:** The wallet stack (07-wallet) sorted by closeness to a reward, so the one that matters is on top. The sign-in footer (04-sign-in) says plainly the email is used only for login, which is rare and honest.
- **Disliked:** The "ההטבה מומשה" screen (21-redeem-done) is a lime circle with no icon or animation; it looks like an asset failed to load at the exact moment that should feel like a win. The הטבות tab icon is just a filled dot, which reads as a placeholder next to the other tab icons.
- **Confused by:** I typed my phone number (05-sign-in-phone-typed) and only got a small hint under the field; nothing told me phone sign-in simply isn't offered.
- **Missing:** Apple Wallet / Google Wallet pass instead of yet another app.
- **Would change:** Give the reward-redeemed screen a real moment (check mark, stamp animation, business name big). Detect a phone number in the email field and say so explicitly.
- **Would use again:** maybe. Only if the café I already go to daily is on it; I will not change routine for a stamp.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay; irrelevant at this price point. Would uninstall if it starts sending promotional pushes. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `redeem-done-screen-empty`, `rewards-tab-placeholder-icon`, `phone-number-not-accepted`, `no-native-wallet-pass`
- **Praise tags:** `wallet-stack-design`, `honest-privacy-copy`

### W-02 · Ruth Feldman, 71, retired barrister (olah from London) · customer · tech 2 · cautious
- **Profile:** Widow, lives in a large flat in the German Colony, financially comfortable. Hebrew is good for the market but slow for reading; English is her phone language. Uses WhatsApp and the bank app, little else.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use)
- **First impression:** Bright and cheerful, but every screen is dense Hebrew and I had to read the sign-in page twice.
- **Liked:** The PIN pad (12-scan-pin) has big, well-spaced buttons and a clear line "הקוד מוקלד על ידי הצוות בקופה", so I know I'm not meant to type. The business page (10-business-page) lays out the rules in a simple table: what earns, what you get, how often.
- **Disliked:** Hebrew only. The legal paragraph under the sign-in button (04-sign-in) is long small print I cannot read comfortably and it implies I'm agreeing to terms I haven't seen. Handing my unlocked phone to a waiter so he can type a code (12-scan-pin) makes me uneasy.

- **Missing:** An English option, or at least larger text settings in the profile (15-profile only offers light/dark).
- **Would change:** Add English. Shorten the sign-in footer to one line with links.
- **Would use again:** maybe. If the bakery girl sets it up for me, yes; on my own, probably not.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay. Would uninstall if it asked for my phone number for marketing or if staff kept fumbling with my phone. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `hebrew-only`, `dense-legal-footer`, `staff-handles-my-phone`, `scan-screen-two-buttons`, `no-text-size-option`
- **Praise tags:** `large-pin-pad`, `clear-business-rules`
