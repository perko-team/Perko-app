# Focus group: LOWER-MIDDLE-INCOME (LM)

### LM-01 · Yossi Ben-Haim, 52, Egged bus driver · customer · tech 2 · skeptical
- **Profile:** Married, three kids, lives in Gilo, traditional (masorti). Buys coffee and a burekas on his break at the same two places, keeps paper punch cards in his shirt pocket. Uses WhatsApp, Waze and the bank app; his email is something his daughter set up years ago and he never checks it.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real counter use)
- **First impression:** "Very yellow." The onboarding cards (01-03) are clear enough, but he wonders why he needs an app for something a piece of cardboard already does.
- **Liked:** The staff PIN pad (12-scan-pin): the cashier does the work, he just turns the phone around; no buttons for him to get wrong. "עוד חותמת אחת להטבה: קפה הפוך עלינו" on the wallet card (07) tells him in one line how close he is.
- **Disliked:** Sign-in (04/05): he typed his phone number 0501234567 and the screen only answered with a small hint "כתובת אימייל, למשל noa@example.com". Every Israeli service asks for a phone number; he would have stopped here. The code screen (06) says to check spam, which he has never done in his life.
- **Confused by:** The neon screen with the big "סריקה" button in the tab bar vs. the "סריקה בקופה" button on the card (08): two ways to the same thing, he does not know which one is "right".
- **Missing:** Sign-in with a phone number and SMS code.
- **Would change:** Put phone/SMS sign-in first; make the text bigger on the sign-in screens, the grey legal paragraph under "שליחת קוד" is tiny.
- **Would use again:** maybe, only if the cashier at his regular place tells him to and helps him set it up.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay a shekel. Uninstalls if it ever asks for the email code again after he loses the phone. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `email-only-signin`, `phone-number-rejected-silently`, `small-legal-text`, `duplicate-scan-entry-points`
- **Praise tags:** `cashier-does-the-pin`, `clear-progress-line`

### LM-02 · Shira Malka, 29, nursery teacher (gannenet) · customer · tech 3 · enthusiastic
- **Profile:** Single, rents a room in Baka, walks Emek Refaim every day after work. Has a drawer full of half-stamped paper cards from cafes she forgot to bring. Pays with her phone, loves anything that organises small things.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (weeks of use)
- **First impression:** "סוף סוף." The line "בלי כרטיס נייר לכל עסק ובלי אפליקציה לכל מועדון" (01) is exactly her problem.
- **Liked:** The wallet stack (07) with the card closest to a reward first, and the "מוכנה למימוש" badge after filling it (16). The "ההטבה מומשה" screen (21) saying a new card already started, so nothing is lost. The rewards tab history (22) with the date, "קפה פינה · 12 בנובמבר 2026".
- **Disliked:** The "card full" sheet (13) and the rewards card (14): ten black circles with a check on only one of them. At a glance it looks like one stamp out of ten, not a full card; she had to read "10/10" to believe it.
- **Confused by:** The card detail (08) shows the card and two buttons and then a big empty area; she expected the history of her stamps there and only sees "פרטי הכרטיס" as a button.
- **Missing:** A way to see, inside the wallet, all businesses near her that she has not added yet beyond the two shown under "עסקים משתתפים שעוד לא בארנק".
- **Would change:** Fill all the circles with the stamp icon (like the coffee cups on 08) when the card is full instead of plain black dots.
- **Would use again:** yes, as long as her regular cafe and bakery join.
- **Would pay / price view:** Expects free; would maybe pay a few shekels a year but would rather not. Uninstalls only if her places never join. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 4
- **Issue tags:** `full-card-looks-empty`, `empty-card-detail-screen`, `want-more-nearby-businesses`
- **Praise tags:** `one-wallet-for-all-cards`, `ready-to-redeem-badge`, `redeemed-history-with-date`, `new-card-auto-starts`

### LM-03 · Eden Cohen, 26, waitress and part-time computer-science student · customer · tech 5 · critical
- **Profile:** Rents in Katamon with her partner; tight budget, splits every bill in Bit. Lives on her phone, tries every new app, switches to dark mode everywhere. Expects a loyalty app to be fast and to not punish her for edge cases.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens)
- **First impression:** Clean, confident design, real Hebrew RTL done right, nice that the onboarding says honestly it is an early pilot (03).
- **Liked:** Dark mode exists and has "לפי הטלפון" (15). The business page (10) spells out the rules, including "תדירות: צבירה אחת בכל שעתיים", so there are no surprises. "מחיקת החשבון" is right there in the profile with a clear warning.
- **Disliked:** Scanning a card that is already full (17) just says "הכרטיס כבר מלא, קודם מממשים": she bought something and that purchase earned nothing. Dark mode wallet (18): the Zaatar card stays bright white and the Dafdouf card stays lime, so the screen flashes at night.
- **Confused by:** The scan screen (11) offers "סריקת קוד לדוגמה" next to the camera button; outside the demo she would not know if she is scanning for real.
- **Missing:** Adding the card to Apple/Google Wallet so she does not have to open the app at the counter.
- **Would change:** When a full card is scanned, redeem and stamp the new card in one step with one PIN instead of losing the purchase.
- **Would use again:** maybe, depends on whether real businesses near her join.
- **Would pay / price view:** Free or nothing. Uninstalls if a stamp is ever lost because of a bug or a cooldown she did not understand. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `full-card-scan-loses-stamp`, `dark-mode-bright-cards`, `demo-scan-button-confusing`, `no-native-wallet-pass`
- **Praise tags:** `clear-business-rules`, `dark-mode-option`, `easy-account-deletion`

### LM-07 · Nissim Dahan, 47, falafel stand owner (one employee) on Derech Beit Lehem · owner · tech 2 · traditional
- **Profile:** Has run the stand for 15 years with one worker, Mohammed. Busy lunch rush from 12 to 2, hands always wet with tahini. Knows his regulars by name; uses a paper card "10th portion free" that costs him almost nothing.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer PIN pad screens 12, 19); HYPOTHETICAL (dashboard, counter use, price)
- **First impression:** "My customers already know me. Why do they need to show me a phone?"
- **Liked:** The PIN idea, so nobody stamps from home with a photo of the QR (the dashboard explains this in the onboarding step). The cooldown in "תוכנית" ("שעתיים", "יום") stops someone buying ten pitas one by one.
- **Disliked:** In the rush, he or Mohammed must stop, wipe hands and type 4 digits on the customer's phone (12) for every portion. The dashboard alert rule "3 קודים שגויים ברצף נחסם בעסק עד סוף היום": if Mohammed mistypes three times, a regular is blocked till tomorrow and Nissim takes the blame.
- **Confused by:** The dashboard asks him to pass the team code "בעל פה, לא בצילום מסך" and change it "מדי פעם"; he does not see why a customer watching would bother.
- **Missing:** A way for him to unblock a customer he knows on the spot (only an alerts list is described).
- **Would change:** Let staff confirm by a tap on their own phone instead of typing on the customer's.
- **Would use again:** maybe, only free and only if customers ask for it.
- **Would pay / price view:** At most 30 ₪ a month, and only if it clearly brings new people from the street; paper cards cost him nearly nothing. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `pin-typing-in-rush`, `lockout-blocks-regulars`, `no-manual-unblock`, `paper-card-cheaper`
- **Praise tags:** `anti-fraud-pin`, `configurable-cooldown`
