# Focus group L: low-income

### L-01 · Yossi Ohana, 58, night security guard (minimum wage) · customer · tech 2 · skeptical
- **Profile:** Lives in Katamonim with his wife, two grown kids still at home. Earns minimum wage on night shifts at a mall entrance, counts every shekel at the supermarket. Uses WhatsApp, the bank app (with his son's help) and the Rav-Kav app; does not really use email. Expects a "club" to give money off, not coffee.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use)
- **First impression:** "עוד מועדון." The bright yellow onboarding looks like an advert; he looked for how much he saves and found a free coffee after 10 coffees.
- **Liked:** The PIN pad screen (12-scan-pin) says plainly "מציגים לקופאי/ת" in big letters, so he knows to hand the phone over and does not have to do anything himself. The "ההטבה מומשה" screen (21) is short and clear.
- **Disliked:** Sign-in (05): he typed his phone number into the box, and the only reaction was small text under it, "כתובת אימייל, למשל noa@example.com". He has an email he never checks and would give up here. The rewards themselves (09-discover): "10 חותמות = קפה הפוך עלינו" means spending around 120-150 ₪ on coffee he would not buy in Emek Refaim anyway.
- **Confused by:** The "card full" sheet (13): ten black circles and one tick in the corner. He thought he had one stamp out of ten, not a full card.
- **Missing:** Sign-in with a phone number and an SMS code. A discount on things he actually buys (bread, cigarettes, groceries) rather than treats.
- **Would change:** Accept a phone number at sign-in. Show the shekel value of each reward on the discover list.
- **Would use again:** no. None of his regular places are in it, and the rewards are for café people.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay anything. Would uninstall the first time it asks for money or shows ads. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `email-only-signin`, `phone-in-email-field-weak-error`, `full-card-visual-unclear`, `rewards-not-everyday-needs`, `reward-value-not-in-shekels`
- **Praise tags:** `clear-hand-to-cashier-screen`, `simple-redeem-confirmation`

### L-02 · Shira Ben-David, 23, social work student on loans, part-time waitress · customer · tech 5 · enthusiastic
- **Profile:** Shares a flat in the German Colony area, lives on a student loan plus tips, about 300 ₪ a month for "going out". Buys a coffee on Emek Refaim most mornings before the bus. Uses Instagram, Wolt, Bit all day; keeps paper punch cards that she always loses.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use, price)
- **First impression:** "סוף סוף." The first onboarding line, "בלי כרטיס נייר לכל עסק ובלי אפליקציה לכל מועדון" (01), is exactly her problem.
- **Liked:** The wallet stack (07) with the card closest to a reward on top and "עוד חותמת אחת להטבה" spelled out, so she knows how close the free coffee is. Rewards tab (22) keeps a dated list of what she redeemed ("קפה פינה · 12 בנובמבר 2026"). Dark mode (18) looks good.
- **Disliked:** Card detail (08): after tapping a card, most of the screen is empty and the other cards are pushed to the bottom edge; "החלקה לצד למעבר בין כרטיסים" is the only hint. The rewards tab icon in the bottom bar is a plain filled circle, which looks unfinished next to the others.
- **Confused by:** The business page (10) says "צבירה אחת בכל שעתיים". If she buys a coffee for a friend too, does only one count?
- **Missing:** A notification or nudge when a new business near her joins. An "invite a friend" option.
- **Would change:** Fill the card detail screen with the card's stamp history right there instead of white space. Explain on the business page what happens when you buy two items in one visit.
- **Would use again:** yes, if her own café joins. It replaces the punch cards she keeps losing.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay; she expects it free like Wolt. Would uninstall if it starts sending marketing spam or asks for card details. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 4
- **Issue tags:** `card-detail-empty-space`, `rewards-tab-icon-unclear`, `cooldown-multi-purchase-unclear`, `no-new-business-alerts`
- **Praise tags:** `replaces-paper-cards`, `progress-to-reward-clear`, `redeem-history`, `dark-mode`

### L-03 · Rivka Weiss, 39, Haredi mother of seven, part-time bookkeeping at home · customer · tech 2 · practical
- **Profile:** Lives in Bayit VeGan, household runs on one small kollel stipend plus her part-time work. Buys challah and rolls in bulk every Thursday. Has one filtered smartphone for work, no social apps, and an email only for work. Shops by price and by hechsher.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use)
- **First impression:** The bakery card in discover (09), "8 חותמות = מאפה לבחירה", caught her eye; the rest (books, flowers, dessert for two at a restaurant) is not her world.
- **Liked:** The privacy line on the scan screen (11), "המצלמה משמשת רק לסריקת קוד ה-QR שבקופה. שום תמונה לא נשמרת", matters to her on a filtered phone. The rules box on the business page (10) lists accrual, reward, redemption and frequency in plain lines.
- **Disliked:** The business page (10) shows hours and address but nothing about kashrut; for a bakery or restaurant that is the first thing she checks. The bakery reward is one pastry after 8 visits, while she buys 300 ₪ at once on Thursday and would still get one stamp.
- **Confused by:** "רכישה מזכה" (02, 10) is never defined for her; does a big order count more than a single roll?
- **Missing:** Kashrut information per business. Rewards that scale with how much you spend, not only per visit.
- **Would change:** Add a kashrut line on the business page. Let points cards (like the bookshop's "200 נק׳") be used by bakeries too so large family orders count.
- **Would use again:** maybe. Only for the bakery, and only if it is kosher and the reward is worth it for a big family.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay. Would uninstall if it showed images or ads she cannot control on her filtered phone. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `no-kashrut-info`, `per-visit-not-per-amount`, `qualifying-purchase-undefined`, `few-relevant-businesses`
- **Praise tags:** `camera-privacy-statement`, `clear-business-rules`

### L-04 · Galina Petrova, 72, pensioner on basic old-age allowance · customer · tech 1 · wary
- **Profile:** Immigrated from Kharkiv in the 1990s, lives alone in a small rented flat off Derech Beit Lehem on the old-age allowance and a rent subsidy. Reads Hebrew slowly, prefers Russian. Her granddaughter set up WhatsApp on a cheap Android phone with a cracked screen. Buys flowers once a month for her husband's grave.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use)
- **First impression:** Too much bright yellow and too many words. She did not know where to press after onboarding.
- **Liked:** The big digits on the PIN pad (12, 19) are easy to see. The flower shop card in discover (09), "5 חותמות = זר קטן", is something she would actually use.
- **Disliked:** The light-grey small text everywhere, for example the long legal paragraph under "שליחת קוד" (04) and the "החלקה לצד למעבר בין כרטיסים" hint (08); she could not read it without glasses. Hebrew only: no Russian at all. Sign-in by email (04): she has an email only on paper, written by her granddaughter.
- **Confused by:** "כניסה" stays grey on the code screen (06) until a code is typed; she thought the app was broken. The greeting "לילה טוב" on the wallet (07) when she opened it in the evening made her think she was being told to go to sleep.
- **Missing:** Russian (or at least larger text). A way to sign in without email.
- **Would change:** Larger, darker body text. A language option.
- **Would use again:** no, not alone. Maybe if the florist's staff did everything for her at the counter.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would never pay. Would uninstall (or ask her granddaughter to) if it fills the phone memory or sends messages in a language she cannot read. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 1
- **Issue tags:** `hebrew-only`, `small-low-contrast-text`, `email-only-signin`, `disabled-button-unclear`, `not-for-elderly`
- **Praise tags:** `large-pin-keypad`, `florist-reward-useful`

### L-07 · Shimon Peretz, 64, kiosk owner on Derech Beit Lehem · owner · tech 2 · traditional
- **Profile:** Has run the same pitzutziya for 30 years, works alone 7 am to 11 pm, margins on cigarettes and drinks are tiny and he is behind on the rent. Customers are regulars he knows by name. Has a smartphone for WhatsApp and the supplier orders; no computer, no printer.
- **Basis:** HYPOTHETICAL (dashboard, real-world use, price); REAL (customer app screens he was shown)
- **First impression:** "אני מכיר את כל הלקוחות שלי, למה אני צריך אפליקציה?" Then he saw the staff PIN pad (12) and asked who exactly is supposed to type it when he is alone with a queue.
- **Liked:** The rule on the business page (10), "צבירה אחת בכל שעתיים", means someone cannot stamp ten times in a minute. The dashboard (from text) says Perko is free in early access.
- **Disliked:** The setup ends with "מדפיסים בגודל מקורי (100%)" a PDF counter card; he has no printer and no computer. Taking each customer's phone to type a PIN (12, 19) with cigarettes, change and a lottery queue is one more step per sale.
- **Confused by:** Dashboard "תוכנית": what counts as "רכישה מזכה" at a kiosk where a sale is a 6 ₪ drink? He would have to decide that himself.
- **Missing:** A printed counter card sent to him by Perko. A way to give the reward without touching the customer's phone.
- **Would change:** Mail or hand-deliver the QR card. Let a regular stamp without a PIN for very small sales.
- **Would use again:** no. It costs him time at the counter and his regulars do not need it to come back.
- **Would pay / price view:** 0 ₪. Even 30 ₪ a month is more than the extra profit he imagines from it. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
