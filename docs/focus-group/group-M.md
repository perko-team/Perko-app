# Focus group M (middle-income)

### M-01 · Michal Ben-Ami, 41, primary-school teacher · customer · tech 3 · enthusiastic
- **Profile:** Teaches 3rd grade, three kids, secular, lives in the German Colony. Household budget is tight but stable; she buys coffee and pastries on Emek Refaim several times a week and has a wallet stuffed with punch cards she always forgets. Uses WhatsApp, Waze and the school's parent app daily.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use over weeks)
- **First impression:** "סוף סוף, בלי עשרה כרטיסי נייר בארנק." The onboarding (01-03) explains the idea in three short sentences she understood immediately.
- **Liked:** The wallet stack (07) showing "9/10" and "עוד חותמת אחת להטבה: קפה הפוך עלינו" right on the card, so she knows how close she is without opening anything. The "ההטבה מומשה ... כרטיס חדש כבר התחיל בארנק" message (21) reassures her the new card started by itself. The "מומשו" history with a date in the rewards tab (22).
- **Disliked:** Sign-in is email only (04); she instinctively typed her phone number (05) and only then saw the hint "כתובת אימייל, למשל". Her school email is full of spam, and the code screen (06) already warns to check spam.
- **Confused by:** The card detail screen (08) shows one card, two buttons and a huge empty area; she expected to see when she got each stamp.
- **Missing:** A way to add a card she already has on paper (her stamps at the bakery would be lost).
- **Would change:** Let her sign in with a phone number and SMS code. Fill the card-detail screen with the stamp history and the business's rules.
- **Would use again:** yes, if the café and bakery she actually visits join.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay; free is the whole point. Would uninstall if her regular places never show up in "גילוי". (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 4
- **Issue tags:** `email-only-signin`, `phone-in-email-field`, `empty-card-detail`, `no-paper-card-migration`
- **Praise tags:** `progress-on-card`, `auto-new-card`, `redeemed-history`

### M-02 · Yossi Mizrahi, 52, clerk at a government office · customer · tech 2 · skeptical
- **Profile:** Civil servant, masorti, married, two grown kids, lives in Katamon. Buys falafel and coffee near work, pays by card and keeps receipts. His phone has a handful of apps the kids installed; he distrusts anything that wants his email.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use at a counter)
- **First impression:** "עוד אפליקציה בשביל קפה חינם?" He does not see why a punch card needs an account, an email and a code.
- **Liked:** The business page (10) lists the rules plainly: "חותמת על כל ארוחה במקום", "צבירה אחת בכל שעתיים". He likes knowing exactly what counts.
- **Disliked:** The PIN pad (12, 19) means handing his phone to the cashier to type on it; he does not hand his phone to strangers. The sign-in (04-06) asks for email plus a 6-digit code just to collect a stamp.
- **Confused by:** On "הכרטיס מלא" (13) all ten circles are black and only one has a check mark; he could not tell which stamps were "his".
- **Missing:** A plain explanation, before sign-in, of who sees his email and data; the footer text on 04 mentions "ספק הענן שלנו", which worried him more.
- **Would change:** Let the cashier scan something from his phone instead of typing on it.
- **Would use again:** no. A paper card does the same job without giving his phone or email away.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would never pay. Would uninstall the first time a cashier fumbles the PIN while a queue waits. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `staff-types-on-my-phone`, `signin-too-heavy`, `stamp-grid-unclear`, `cloud-storage-worry`
- **Praise tags:** `clear-business-rules`

### M-03 · Avi Shapira, 33, backend developer at a mid-size tech company · customer · tech 5 · tech-savvy
- **Profile:** Lives with his partner in Baka, works hybrid, gets two coffees a day near home. Uses Apple Wallet for everything, notices UI details, reads privacy policies sometimes. Expects slick, fast, native-feeling apps.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (security in real use)
- **First impression:** Clean brand, strong lime-and-ink look, well-written Hebrew copy. Feels like a real product, not a municipal app.
- **Liked:** The wallet stack sorted with the card closest to a reward first (07), and the "מוכנה למימוש" pill appearing after the card filled (16). The second scan of a full card (17) correctly says "הכרטיס כבר מלא" and offers redemption instead of failing silently. Dark mode (18-22) is consistent.
- **Disliked:** Several screens feel unfinished: the card detail (08) is mostly empty space, the rewards tab icon is a plain filled circle, and "ההטבה מומשה" (21) shows a lime circle with no icon inside. The demo PIN is 1234 and the staff types it on the customer's screen in full view (12): anyone watching learns it.
- **Confused by:** "החלקה לצד למעבר בין כרטיסים" on 08: the swipe hint and the peeking "דפדוף" card at the bottom overlap the tab bar.
- **Missing:** Adding the card to Apple/Google Wallet.
- **Would change:** Put the stamp history and rules into the card-detail screen. Give the success screen (21) an actual check icon and a bit of animation.
- **Would use again:** yes, it is better than paper; he would keep it if his café joins.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay as a customer. Would uninstall if it starts sending marketing push notifications. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 4
- **Issue tags:** `empty-card-detail`, `placeholder-icons`, `pin-shoulder-surfing`, `swipe-hint-overlap`, `no-native-wallet`
- **Praise tags:** `closest-reward-first`, `full-card-handling`, `dark-mode`, `polished-copy`

- **Would change:** Show the earning rate for points programs ("נקודה לכל X ₪"). Mark every collected stamp with a check, not only the last one.
- **Would use again:** maybe, at the bookshop and bakery if the reward is real value.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay a single shekel; she'd uninstall if any fee or subscription appeared. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `points-rate-unclear`, `stamp-grid-unclear`, `dense-text`, `hebrew-only`, `cooldown-rule-unclear`

- **First impression:** "למה העובד שלי צריך לגעת בטלפון של הלקוח?" The PIN pad on the customer's phone (12) looks slow for a Friday queue of 30 people.
