# Focus group: UPPER-MIDDLE-INCOME (UM)

### UM-01 · Daniel Mizrahi, 38, senior backend engineer · customer · tech 5 · skeptical
- **Profile:** Works at a Har Hotzvim tech company, lives in Baka with his wife and two kids, secular. Buys coffee daily on Emek Refaim and pays with his phone. Expects a loyalty app to be faster than a paper card, or he won't bother.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use at a counter)
- **First impression:** Clean, confident design, and the email code sign-in with no password is the right call. But I'm watching for where it gets slower than a paper card.
- **Liked:** Staff PIN pad (12-scan-pin): having staff confirm on my phone means a photo of the counter QR can't be used to collect stamps from home. Sensible anti-fraud with no extra hardware. Wallet (07) sorts the card closest to a reward to the top, which is the one I actually need.
- **Disliked:** Card detail (08): one card fills the top third and the rest is empty space, with the next card peeking at the bottom. The swipe hint "החלקה לצד למעבר בין כרטיסים" is doing a job the layout should do. Staff PIN pad (12/20): staff type a 4-digit code on *my* screen in front of me, so anyone who watches a few times learns it. The PIN protects against the photo but not against shoulder-surfing.
- **Confused by:** Wallet stack (07): the middle card (זעתר) shows only its name and "4/6". I wasn't sure whether tapping it expands it or opens the detail view.
- **Missing:** An Apple Wallet / Google Wallet pass, so I could skip opening another app at the counter.
- **Would change:** Make the card detail screen show the stamp history and rules right under the card instead of empty space. Let the wallet card open straight to the scanner with one tap.
- **Would use again:** maybe. Only if two or three places I already go join. Five sample businesses marked "לדוגמה" give me no reason yet.
- **Would pay / price view:** Nothing; this should be free. I'd uninstall it if it starts sending promo push notifications or asks for my phone contacts. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `pin-shoulder-surfing`, `card-detail-empty-space`, `wallet-stack-unclear-tap`, `no-native-wallet-pass`, `few-real-businesses`
- **Praise tags:** `staff-pin-anti-fraud`, `closest-reward-first`, `passwordless-email-code`

### UM-02 · Dr. Michal Ben-Ami, 44, family physician · customer · tech 3 · impatient
- **Profile:** Runs a busy clinic, lives in the German Colony with three kids and a husband who travels. Buys bread and pastries on the way home in five-minute windows. Wants zero setup; anything that slows a queue is a dealbreaker.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens); HYPOTHETICAL (real-world use at a counter)
- **First impression:** Three onboarding screens before I can do anything. Luckily there's "דילוג". Then it wants my email when I expected my phone number.
- **Liked:** "דילוג" on onboarding (01-02) is visible and immediate. Rewards tab after redeeming (22): the redeemed coffee with the date ("12 בנובמבר 2026") gives me a clean record and no clutter.
- **Disliked:** Sign-in (05): I typed my phone number out of habit. The app only showed a small grey hint, "כתובת אימייל, למשל…", and the "שליחת קוד" button still looked active. In Israel everything signs in by SMS. Code screen (06): "כדאי לבדוק גם בספאם" plus a 29-second resend timer means digging through my inbox at the counter. Scan → PIN → result (11-13) is three screens, and handing my phone to the cashier is one more thing in a rush.
- **Confused by:** PIN screen (12): "(9 מתוך 10)". Is this stamp number 9, or will I be at 10 after it? I had 9 already.
- **Missing:** Phone/SMS sign-in.
- **Would change:** Accept a phone number at sign-in, or reject it clearly with a real error message. Put the stamp count after confirmation in the PIN text ("זו החותמת ה-10").
- **Would use again:** maybe. Only for the bakery I go to three times a week, and only if staff are quick with the PIN.
- **Would pay / price view:** Free, obviously. I'd uninstall if a stamp ever takes longer than paying. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `email-only-signin`, `phone-input-weak-error`, `email-code-spam-friction`, `too-many-steps-at-counter`, `pin-count-wording-unclear`
- **Praise tags:** `skippable-onboarding`, `redemption-history-dated`

### UM-03 · Adv. Yossi Halevi, 56, real-estate lawyer · customer · tech 2 · traditional
- **Profile:** Partner in a small firm, lives in Talbiyeh, dati-leumi, married with five grown or teenage kids. Keeps paper punch cards in his wallet and his secretary handles anything digital. Reads every word of a terms screen out of habit.
- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens)
- **First impression:** Bright yellow-green, pleasant enough, but I don't see what it does that a cardboard card in my wallet doesn't.
- **Liked:** Business page rules box (10): "צבירה / הטבה / מימוש / תדירות" sets out the terms plainly, like a short contract. Delete-account section (15) says plainly that it can't be undone. Honest.
- **Disliked:** Sign-in footer (04): "נשמרת אצל ספק הענן שלנו". Which provider, and in which country? As a lawyer I want that named. The constant "מצב הדגמה" box on every tab (07, 09, 14, 15) plus "לדוגמה" tags on every card makes the whole thing feel provisional.
- **Confused by:** Business page (10): hours show only "א׳-ה׳ 12:00-23:00". Is it closed Friday, or is Friday just not listed? For me Friday and Motzaei Shabbat matter.
- **Missing:** Kashrut information on the business page; for a restaurant like זעתר that's the first thing I check.
- **Would change:** Name the cloud provider and data location in the footer or privacy link. Add kashrut and Friday/Motzaei Shabbat hours to the business page.
- **Would use again:** no. Paper works, and I don't want the cashier holding my phone.
- **Would pay / price view:** Would not pay. Would uninstall the moment it asked for anything beyond an email. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 2
- **Issue tags:** `cloud-provider-unnamed`, `demo-banner-everywhere`, `incomplete-hours`, `no-kashrut-info`, `prefers-paper`
- **Praise tags:** `clear-business-rules`, `honest-account-deletion`

### UM-04 · Noa Peretz-Levin, 31, product manager · customer · tech 5 · enthusiastic

I had to go back to the scan flow to redeem, and the tab whose name means "rewards" should be where I redeem. Redeem done (21): the success icon is just a plain lime circle with no check mark, which feels unfinished next to the polished rest. The Rewards tab icon in the bottom bar is a plain dot as well.
- **Confused by:** Card detail (08): the last stamp slot shows a check outline even though it's empty. Is that a "goal" marker or did something already happen?
- **Missing:** A nudge when I'm one stamp away (push notifications weren't shown here, so I don't know if they exist).
- **Would change:** Add a "למימוש" button on the Rewards tab card. Put a check mark in the success circle on 21.
- **Would use again:** yes. If my café and the bakery join I'm in on day one.
- **Would pay / price view:** Wouldn't pay; customers shouldn't. I'd uninstall if businesses started pushing ads through it. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 4
- **Issue tags:** `rewards-tab-no-redeem-button`, `success-icon-blank`, `rewards-tab-icon-generic`, `empty-slot-check-ambiguous`, `wants-near-reward-nudge`

- **Basis:** REAL (customer app screens)

- **Confused by:** Wallet (07): "120 נק׳" with a progress bar, but the text "עוד 80 נק׳" and the bar don't give a sense of how many purchases are left.
- **Missing:** Arabic (and English) language option.
- **Would change:** Show "X נק׳ על כל רכישה" next to the points reward on discover and the business page.
- **Would use again:** maybe. For the café, yes; the points card I'd ignore until the rate is clear.
- **Would pay / price view:** Free only. I'd uninstall if the rewards turn out to be smaller than they look. (HYPOTHETICAL)
- **Satisfaction:** 3
- **Issue tags:** `points-earn-rate-missing`, `hebrew-only`, `points-progress-unclear`
- **Praise tags:** `reward-equation-clear`, `cooldown-shown-upfront`
